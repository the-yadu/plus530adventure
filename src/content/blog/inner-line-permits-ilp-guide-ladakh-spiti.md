# Inner Line Permits (ILP) & Protected Area Permits (PAP) Explained for Leh-Ladakh & Spiti

Traversing high-altitude Trans-Himalayan corridors like **Pangong Tso, Nubra Valley, Hanle, Chushul, and Umling La** takes overlanders close to international borders. To ensure national security and visitor safety, the Union Territory of Ladakh and the state of Himachal Pradesh enforce strict permit systems.

Here is everything you need to know about applying for **Inner Line Permits (ILP)** and **Protected Area Permits (PAP)**.

---

## 1. What is an Inner Line Permit (ILP)?

An Inner Line Permit (ILP) is an official travel document issued by government authorities allowing Indian citizens to visit protected border areas.

Foreign passport holders (including OCI cardholders) require a **Protected Area Permit (PAP)**, which must be issued through registered local travel agencies for groups of two or more travelers.

---

## 2. Protected Regions Requiring Permits in Ladakh

You must hold a valid permit to visit any of the following regions beyond Leh town:

- **Nubra Valley**: Diskit, Hunder, Sumur, Panamik, Turtuk, Tyakshi.
- **Pangong Tso Circuit**: Durbuk, Tangtse, Spangmik, Man, Merak.
- **Changthang Plateau**: Chushul, Tsaga La, Loma, Nyoma, Hanle, Tso Moriri.
- **Extreme Passes**: Khardung La, Chang La, Umling La, Marsimik La.

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Online Application Process (Ladakh)

### Step 1: Portal Registration
Visit the official UT Ladakh permit portal: `lahdclehpermit.in`.

### Step 2: Select Category & Circuit
Select **Indian Domestic Tourist** or **Foreign Tourist**. Choose your intended travel circuits (e.g., *Nubra - Pangong - Hanle - Tso Moriri loop*).

### Step 3: Upload Documents
- Government Photo ID (Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Voter ID).
- Vehicle Registration details for self-drive expeditions.

### Step 4: Pay Required Fees
- **Environment Fee**: ₹400 per person.
- **Wildlife Protection Fee**: ₹20 per person per day.
- **Red Cross Donation**: ₹50 per person.

### Step 5: Print Physical Copies
Once approved, print **at least 8 to 10 physical copies** of your signed permit. Electronic copies on smartphones are **not accepted** at remote military checkposts.

---

## 4. Military Checkpost Protocols & Etiquette

You will encounter Indian Army and ITBP checkposts at strategic locations (such as Karu, North Pullu, South Pullu, Durbuk, Loma, and Hanle).

- Keep physical permit copies and original photo IDs readily accessible in your dashboard glovebox.
- At each checkpost, hand over one printed copy per vehicle.
- Photography of military installations, radar towers, and army camps is strictly prohibited.

## Permit Comparison Table

| Feature | Indian Domestic Citizens (ILP) | Foreign Passport Holders (PAP) |
|---|---|---|
| **Portal / Issuer** | Online (`lahdclehpermit.in`) | Local Registered Agency / DC Office |
| **Minimum Group** | 1 Person | 2 Persons Minimum |
| **Valid Duration** | Up to 15 Days | Up to 7 Days (Extendable) |
| **Physical Copies** | 8-10 Copies Required | 8-10 Copies Required |
| **Restricted Zones** | Hanle & Umling La Permitted | Hanle / Umling La restricted to select PAP |
