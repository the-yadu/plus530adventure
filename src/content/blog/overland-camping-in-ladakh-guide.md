# Overland Camping in Ladakh: Top Pristine Spots, Leave-No-Trace & High-Altitude Gear

Waking up inside a rooftop tent to the reflection of snow-capped peaks on the turquoise waters of Pangong Tso is the ultimate wilderness dream for overlanders. The vast, high-altitude cold desert of Ladakh offers unmatched wilderness camping opportunities.

However, camping at 14,000 feet requires specialized sub-zero sleeping gear, extreme wind protection, and strict adherence to environmental preservation laws. Here is your complete guide to overland camping in Ladakh.

---

## 1. Top Overland Camping Locations in Ladakh

### A. Pangong Tso (Spangmik, Man & Merak Villages)
- **Elevation**: 13,860 feet
- **Highlight**: Camping near the world's highest saltwater lake. To protect the fragile shore ecosystem, camp in designated eco-zones in Man or Merak villages, at least 200 meters away from the water edge.

### B. Tso Moriri & Korzok
- **Elevation**: 14,836 feet
- **Highlight**: A tranquil freshwater lake surrounded by snow-covered peaks. Rich in birdlife, including the endangered Black-necked Crane.

### C. Hanle Dark Sky Reserve
- **Elevation**: 14,764 feet
- **Highlight**: Home to the Indian Astronomical Observatory and Asia's first designated Dark Sky Reserve. Experience breathtaking views of the Milky Way galaxy.

### D. Hunder Sand Dunes, Nubra Valley
- **Elevation**: 10,000 feet
- **Highlight**: Milder nighttime temperatures, sea-buckthorn bushes, and dramatic white sand dunes with double-humped Bactrian camels.

## 2. High-Altitude Sub-Zero Camping Gear Guide

At 14,000 feet, night temperatures regularly plunge below freezing (-5°C to -15°C), even in summer.

- **4-Season Expedition Tent**: Standard summer tents will collapse under gale-force Himalayan winds. Use heavy-duty 4-season aluminum-pole geodesic tents or hard-shell rooftop tents with insulated walls.
- **Sub-Zero Sleeping Bags**: Choose goose down or high-grade synthetic sleeping bags rated for **Comfort temp -10°C to -20°C**.
- **Insulated Sleeping Pads**: Ground cold causes rapid hypothermia. Use closed-cell foam or insulated inflatable sleeping pads with an **R-value rating of 4.5 or higher**.
- **Wind-Proof Cooking Stoves**: Standard butane canister stoves freeze in low temperatures. Use multi-fuel stoves (running on petrol/diesel) or pressurized liquid fuel stoves equipped with folding aluminum wind deflectors.

---

## 3. Leave-No-Trace (LNT) Principles for Ladakh

Ladakh's high-altitude cold desert ecosystem decomposes organic waste extremely slowly. Careless disposal permanently scars the landscape.

1. **Pack It In, Pack It Out**: Carry heavy-duty trash bags inside your vehicle. Every piece of food wrapping, bottle cap, and tissue paper must return with you to Leh for urban waste disposal.
2. **Never Dump Greywater Near Lakes**: Wash dishes at least 200 feet away from lakes and streams using bio-degradable, eco-friendly soap.
3. **No Off-Track Driving**: Driving off designated trails crushes delicate tundra soil, destroying wildlife nesting grounds. Always stay on established dirt tracks.
4. **Respect Local Communities & Monasteries**: Ask permission before setting up camp near villages or prayer flags. Avoid loud music or campfires using scarce local firewood.
